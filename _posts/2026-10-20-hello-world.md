Welcome! This is the very first post on my new blog. 🎉

I've wanted a place to write things down for a long time — half-finished ideas,
notes on things I've learned, and the occasional longer essay. Social media is
too fleeting for that, so here we are: a small, fast, free website that I fully own.

## Why write in public?

> The best way to learn something is to explain it to someone else.

Writing forces me to actually understand a topic instead of just *feeling* like I do.
And if a post helps even one other person get unstuck, that's a bonus.

## What's coming

- Notes from projects I'm working on
- Short tutorials for things I had to figure out the hard way
- Thoughts on tools, workflows, and learning

Thanks for stopping by — see you in the next post.
